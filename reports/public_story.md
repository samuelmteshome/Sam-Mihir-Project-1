# Clinical Trial Deserts: Are Stroke Trials Located Where Stroke Burden Is Highest?

**Draft outline — findings are not yet available.**

Audience: people interested in stroke care, community health organizations, and
public health planners. Explain how the geography of clinical research relates
to community health, without suggesting that a county-level measure establishes
any person's eligibility or access to treatment.

## Opening

Introduce the question: do counties with higher modeled stroke prevalence also
have recorded stroke trial sites? Explain the selected recruitment/infrastructure
definition and why the distinction matters.

## Takeaway and limitations

Write a three-sentence takeaway grounded in the figures. Include modeled
prevalence, differing data years, incomplete/stale registry records, site status,
coordinate uncertainty, and travel across county borders. Use “potential access
mismatch” rather than claiming that a community has no possible trial access or
needs a particular intervention. Add source citations and a link to the repo.
